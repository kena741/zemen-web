Pick the Right Trade

  • Electricians for wiring, sockets and lighting
  • Plumbers for pipes, taps and sanitary installation
  • Appliance repair for fridges, ovens and washing machines
  • AC repair and service
  • CCTV installation and IT support

What to Check Before You Book

  • Identity and experience in that trade
  • A price, or how the price is worked out, before work starts
  • Whether parts and materials are included
  • What happens if the fault comes back

Describe the Problem Clearly

Send photos and a short description when you book. A technician who understands the problem can bring the right tools and parts the first time.
