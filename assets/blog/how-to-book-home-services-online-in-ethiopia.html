To book a home service online in Ethiopia, choose the service on a booking platform, compare providers and prices, pick a time, and confirm the booking. On Zemen Service you can do this on the website or the Zemen Service app, or call 8323 and the team books for you.

Step by Step on Zemen Service

  1. Go to all services and choose a category, such as cleaning, cooking or plumbing.
  2. Compare listings. Each one shows the provider's price and what the service includes.
  3. Choose a listing and book it.
  4. The provider does the job, and the support team is available if you need help.

Book in the App

The Zemen Service app is on Google Play and the App Store.

Prefer to Call?

Call 8323, or message on WhatsApp, and the team will book for you.

Common Questions

Which areas can I book in?

Zemen Service providers work across Addis Ababa.

Are the providers verified?

Yes. Providers' IDs and documents are reviewed before they can take jobs, and each listing is reviewed before it is published.

Can I see prices before I book?

Yes. Listing prices are shown on each service page.
