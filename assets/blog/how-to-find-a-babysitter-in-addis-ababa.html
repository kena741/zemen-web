To find a babysitter in Addis Ababa, decide what care you need, look for someone with verified identity and childcare experience, meet them with your child before the first booking, and agree the rules in writing. On Zemen Service you can compare babysitting listings from verified providers and book online or by calling 8323.

Decide What Kind of Care You Need

  • Occasional babysitting: for an evening out or an event.
  • Regular daytime care: a nanny who comes on set days.
  • Specialist care: for a baby, or for a child with a disability or medical needs.

The type of care changes who you should look for and what you should ask.

What to Check Before You Book

  • Identity: a government ID such as the Fayda National ID.
  • Experience: with children of the same age as yours.
  • Health: on Zemen Service, childcare providers must complete a medical checkup.
  • References: previous families or employers you can contact.

Questions to Ask a Babysitter

  • What would you do if my child had a fever or an accident?
  • How do you handle a child who will not sleep or eat?
  • Are you comfortable with our rules on screens, food and visitors?
  • Can you stay late if we are delayed?

Agree the Details in Writing

Write down the hours, the pay, emergency contacts, allergies and medicines, and any house rules. Leave the list where the babysitter can see it.

Common Questions

How do I find a trusted babysitter in Addis Ababa?

Use a service that verifies identity and experience, meet the babysitter with your child first, and check references. Browse babysitting services on Zemen Service.

Are Zemen Service babysitters checked?

Providers submit their Fayda National ID and the documents for their services for review, and childcare providers also complete a medical checkup.

Can I book a babysitter for one evening?

Many providers offer one-time bookings. Check the listing or call 8323 to ask.
