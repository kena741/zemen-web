Choose the Right Type of Cooking Help

  • A home cook: cooks everyday meals in your kitchen on set days.
  • A private chef: for special dinners, guests or a particular cuisine.
  • Meal plans: meals prepared and delivered, including fasting meals.
  • Event catering: for weddings, birthdays and office events.

What to Check

  • Experience with the cuisine you want, Ethiopian, international or both.
  • Food hygiene: ask how they store and handle food.
  • Whether they can cook for fasting days or for allergies.
  • A trial meal before a regular arrangement.

Agree the Practical Details

  • Who buys the ingredients and how they are paid for
  • How many people they cook for, and how often
  • Whether cleaning the kitchen afterward is included
