To hire office cleaning in Addis Ababa, define the areas and how often they need cleaning, ask providers what equipment and supplies they bring, agree a schedule that does not disrupt work, and start with a trial clean. Offices can book verified cleaners on Zemen Service the same way households do, or call 8323 for help with a larger job.

Define the Scope

  • The rooms: offices, meeting rooms, kitchen, bathrooms, reception
  • How often: daily, weekly, or a one-time deep clean
  • Special work: floor scrubbing, sofa and carpet cleaning, windows

Questions to Ask a Cleaning Provider

  • Do you bring cleaning supplies and machines, such as floor scrubbers?
  • How many cleaners will come, and for how long?
  • Can you clean outside working hours?
  • Who do we contact if something is missed?
