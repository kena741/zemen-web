Before you hire a domestic worker in Ethiopia, check four things: their identity, their references or work history, exactly what the job includes, and how you will both handle problems. Hiring through a platform that verifies providers, such as Zemen Service, covers the identity and document checks for you, but you should still agree the details of the job yourself.

1. Confirm Their Identity

Ask to see a government ID. Ethiopia's national digital ID, Fayda, is issued by the National ID Program, and many people now have one. Make sure the name and photo match the person in front of you.

On Zemen Service, every provider uploads the front and back of their Fayda National ID, and the Zemen team reviews it before the provider can take jobs.

2. Ask About Experience and References

  • Where have they worked before, and for how long?
  • Can you speak to a previous employer?
  • Do they have training for the work, for example in childcare or cooking?

Zemen Service asks providers for the documents each service category needs, such as proof of training or work history, and reviews them before approving their listings.

3. Agree What the Job Includes

Most disagreements start with unclear expectations. Write down:

  • The tasks, such as cleaning, laundry, cooking or childcare
  • The days and hours
  • Whether they live in or come daily
  • The pay, when it is paid, and what it covers
  • Who provides cleaning supplies and equipment

4. Extra Checks for Childcare

If the person will look after children, ask about their experience with children of the same age, and about first aid. On Zemen Service, providers offering childcare must also complete a medical checkup.

5. Plan How You Will Handle Problems

Agree how either side can raise a concern or end the arrangement. When you book through Zemen Service, our support team is available by calling 8323 if something goes wrong.
