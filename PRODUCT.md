## Product

Zemen is a home-services marketplace (web) for customers and providers in Ethiopia. Providers create and manage services, bookings, wallet, and team; customers book and pay.

## Users

- Providers listing services and fulfilling bookings
- Customers discovering and booking local services

## Personality

Trustworthy, grounded, green-forward (brand `#174309`). Clear task UI over decoration. Amharic + English.

## Anti-references

Generic shadcn form stacks, purple gradients, cream/terracotta “AI SaaS” looks, dense nested cards.
